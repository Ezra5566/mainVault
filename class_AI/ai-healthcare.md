## Overview
Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare by enabling data‑driven insights, automating routine tasks, and supporting clinical decision‑making.

## Key Use‑Cases
- **Medical Imaging**: CNN‑based detection of pathologies in X‑rays, MRIs, CT scans (e.g., lung nodule detection).  
- **Predictive Analytics**: Forecasting patient deterioration, readmission risk, and disease onset.  
- **Personalized Treatment**: Recommending dosage adjustments based on genetics and comorbidities.  
- **Drug Discovery**: Generative models designing novel compounds and predicting efficacy.  
- **Virtual Health Assistants**: Chatbots that triage symptoms and provide medication reminders.

## Benefits
- **Improved Accuracy**: Early detection leads to better outcomes.  
- **Operational Efficiency**: Automation reduces administrative burden.  
- **Cost Reduction**: Streamlined workflows lower expenses.

## Challenges & Ethical Considerations
- **Data Privacy**: Protecting sensitive health records.  
- **Bias & Fairness**: Ensuring models don't disadvantage under‑represented groups.  
- **Transparency**: Clinicians need interpretable explanations for AI recommendations.  
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Meeting FDA/EMA standards for medical AI devices.

## Classroom Activities
- **Case Study**: Analyze a published AI‑driven diagnostic study; critique methodology and bias.  
- **Hands‑On**: Use a pre‑trained model to classify skin lesions from a public dataset.  
- **Debate**: Should AI‑generated diagnoses be considered definitive?
