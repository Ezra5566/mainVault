**Definition**  
General AI (also called Strong AI or AGI – Artificial General Intelligence) refers to a hypothetical AI system that can understand, learn, and apply knowledge across **any** intellectual task that a human being can. Unlike narrow AI, a General AI would:

- Possess flexible reasoning and abstract thought.  
- Transfer knowledge from one domain to another without task‑specific retraining.  
- Exhibit self‑awareness, intentionality, and common‑sense understanding.

**Current Status**  
- Still a research goal; no operational AGI exists.  
- Conceptual discussions around safety, alignment, and societal impact.  
- Inspirational for sci‑fi narratives and long‑term AI roadmap planning.

**Implications for Curriculum**  
- Introduced early to set expectations and provoke ethical debates.  
- Serves as a bridge to topics like science‑fiction AI, AI governance, and speculative futures.

**Key Takeaway**  
General AI remains aspirational; understanding its promise and peril helps students critically assess AI claims in media and industry.
