- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** – The broad field of creating systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence.  
- **Machine Learning (ML)** – A subset of AI where systems improve their performance by learning from data rather than being explicitly programmed with static rules.  
- **Deep Learning (DL)** – A further subset of ML that uses multi‑layer neural networks (deep architectures) to automatically learn hierarchical representations of data.

These layers can be visualized as:

```
AI (big umbrella)
 └─ ML (learn from data)
      └─ DL (deep neural networks)
```

Understanding the distinctions helps students choose the right toolset for a given problem.
