# AI Foundations (Weeks 1–2)

**Goal:** Students understand what AI actually is.

## Topics
- **What AI is vs what it is not**
- **AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning**
- **Types of AI**
  - Narrow AI
  - General AI (concept)
  - Generative AI
- **Real‑world applications**
  - Healthcare
  - Finance
  - Education
  - Manufacturing
  - Autonomous systems
- **AI history and current trends**

## Activities
- Students identify AI they already use daily
- Case‑study discussions
