## Overview
Artificial Intelligence is reshaping the financial industry by enabling data‑driven insights, automating complex tasks, and improving risk management.

## Key Use‑Cases
- **Fraud Detection** – Anomaly‑detection models flag suspicious transactions in real time.  
- **Algorithmic Trading** – Predictive models execute trades at optimal moments based on market signals.  
- **Credit Scoring** – Alternative data sources (e.g., utility payments) refine creditworthiness assessments.  
- **Robo‑Advisors** – Portfolio construction and rebalancing driven by reinforcement‑learning algorithms.  
- **Regulatory Compliance** – Automated monitoring of transactions for anti‑money‑laundering (AML) purposes.

## Benefits
- **Speed & Scale** – Processes millions of transactions per second.  
- **Personalization** – Tailors financial products to individual behavior.  
- **Cost Reduction** – Automates routine analyses, lowering operational expenses.  
- **Enhanced Accuracy** – Reduces false positives/negatives in fraud and credit models.

## Challenges & Ethical Considerations
- **Data Privacy** – Handling sensitive financial data requires robust protection.  
- **Model Interpretability** – Regulators demand explainable decisions for credit and compliance.  
- **Bias & Fairness** – Risk of discriminatory outcomes in loan approvals.  
- **Security** – Adversarial attacks targeting model inputs or outputs.

## Classroom Activities
- **Case Study** – Analyze a published AI‑driven fraud detection system and evaluate its performance metrics.  
- **Hands‑On Simulation** – Build a simple credit‑scoring model using synthetic data and evaluate ROC‑AUC.  
- **Debate** – Discuss whether AI‑generated credit scores should be disclosed to applicants.
