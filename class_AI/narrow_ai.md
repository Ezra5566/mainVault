**Definition**  
Narrow AI refers to systems that excel at a *single* class of tasks or a limited set of well‑defined problems. These models are trained for specific functions—such as image classification, language translation, or playing a particular game—and do not possess general reasoning abilities.

**Typical Examples**  
- Recommendation engines (e.g., Netflix, Amazon)  
- Speech recognition (e.g., Siri, Alexa)  
- Autonomous driving perception modules  
- Spam detection filters  

**Key Characteristic**  
- Operates within a *bounded* problem space; performance degrades when faced with concepts outside its training domain.

**Implication for Students**  
- Most AI applications encountered in industry today are narrow AI. Understanding their limitations is crucial for ethical deployment and realistic expectation setting.
