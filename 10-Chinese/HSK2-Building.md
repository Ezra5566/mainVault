# HSK 2 — Building On HSK 1

> [!summary] What this note is
> The grammar HSK 2 adds on top of [[HSK1-Basics]]. HSK 1 gave you the sentence skeleton; HSK 2 (~300 new words, cumulative ~350) adds **tense/aspect markers, possession, the 把/被/比 constructions, and conditionals** — the pieces that let you say *what happened*, not just *what is*.

## 1. The HSK 2 Grammar Core

### 1.1 了 (le) — the aspect marker
The single biggest HSK 2 addition. 了 marks **completion/change of state** and is the Chinese "past." There are two:
- **了₁ after a verb** = "did / have done" (the action completed). 我吃了饭。 = I've eaten.
- **了₂ at the sentence end** = a change happened. 下雨了。 = It's started raining.

```
我昨天去了图书馆。  Wǒ zuótiān qù le túshūguǎn.  I went to the library yesterday.
```
> [!warning] 了 vs 没
> "didn't" is **没 + verb** (never 不 for past). 我没吃饭 = I didn't eat. 我不吃饭 = I don't eat (habit).

### 1.2 的 (de) — the "possessive / modifier" particle
Links a modifier to the noun it modifies.

```
我的书      Wǒ de shū       my book
红色的花    hóngsè de huā  red flowers
中国的老师  Zhōngguó de lǎoshī  a teacher from China
```
> [!note] Three 的: 的 (modifiers/possession), 地 (adverb + verb), 得 (verb + result/degree). HSK 2 mostly uses 的.

### 1.3 把 (bǎ) — "handle/dispose of" construction
`把 + object + verb + result/place`. Use it when the verb has a **result, direction, or location** — it front-loads the object.

```
我把书放在桌子上。  Wǒ bǎ shū fàng zài zhuōzi shàng.
I put the book on the table.  (object 书 before the verb 放)
我把作业交了。  Wǒ bǎ zuòyè jiāo le.  I handed in my homework.
```
> [!tip] When 把 appears
> HSK 2 introduces 把 for "what did you *do with* it." It's the pattern examiners love. Master: 把 + object + verb + 在/到/place.

### 1.4 被 (bèi) — the passive
`A 被 B verb` = "A is verb-ed by B" (A suffers the action).

```
我的钱包被偷了。  Wǒ de qiánbào bèi tōu le.  My wallet was stolen (by someone).
```
> [!note] 被 vs 把
> 把 = active, "you did X to the object." 被 = passive, "the object had X done to it." Same event, opposite focus.

### 1.5 比 (bǐ) — comparison
`A 比 B + adjective` = "A is more [adj] than B." No 更 needed in the 比 form.

```
我比他高。      Wǒ bǐ tā gāo.        I'm taller than him.
这本书比那本贵。  Zhè běn shū bǐ nà běn guì.  This book is pricier than that one.
```
For "less than," use 不比 (bù bǐ) or reverse A and B.

### 1.6 Because & conditionals
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **因为…所以…** (yīnwèi…suǒyǐ) | because… so… | 因为我忙，所以我不去。 |
| **如果…就…** (rúguǒ…jiù) | if… then… | 如果明天好，我们就去公园。 |
| 就 (jiù) | "then / just" (result emphasis) | 如果你有时间，就來我家。 |

In casual speech you can drop 因为 or 就: 我很忙，所以我不去。

### 1.7 Both / simultaneous
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **又…又…** (yòu…yòu) | both… and… (two qualities) | 这个苹果又大又甜。 |
| **一边…一边…** | doing A while doing B | 他一边吃饭，一边看书。 |

### 1.8 Connectors & hedges you'll now use a lot
| Word | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| 也 (yě) | also / too | 我也喜欢苹果。 |
| 都 (dōu) | all / both | 我们都很忙。 |
| 但是 / 不过 | but | 我喜欢茶，但是我不喜欢咖啡。 |
| 和 (hé) | and (nouns only) | 茶和水都是饮料。 |
| 最 (zuì) | most / -est | 今天最热。 |

## 2. HSK 2 Sentence Patterns (memorize these)

```
我昨天去了图书馆。       I went to the library yesterday.
他今天吃了什么？         What did he eat today?
我们下午要去购物。       We're going shopping in the afternoon.
这件衣服很贵。           This piece of clothing is expensive.
他的名字叫李华。         His name is Li Hua.
我们准备去旅行。         We are preparing to travel.
你可以帮我吗？           Can you help me?
这件事我已经知道了。     I already know about this.
我们的计划很完美。       Our plan is perfect.
我喜欢阅读小说。         I like reading novels.
```

> [!tip] The HSK 2 shift in one line
> HSK 1: *what is / what do.*  HSK 2: *what happened, what did you do to it, how does A compare to B, and what will you do if…*

## 3. Longer HSK 2 Dialogues (read + act out)

> [!example] At a restaurant
> A: 你好，我想点菜。 (Ni hǎo, wǒ xiǎng diǎn cài.) Hi, I'd like to order.
> B: 好的。你要什么？ (Hǎo de. Nǐ yào shénme?) Sure. What do you want?
> A: 我要一碗面和一杯茶。 (Wǒ yào yì wǎn miàn hé yì bēi chá.) A bowl of noodles and a cup of tea.
> B: 好的。还需要什么？ (Hǎo de. Hái xūyào shénme?) Anything else?
> A: 谢谢，不需要了。 (Xièxie, bù xūyào le.) Thanks, that's all.

> [!example] Making a plan
> A: 我们周末去公园好吗？ (Wǒmen zhōumò qù gōngyuán hǎo ma?) Shall we go to the park at the weekend?
> B: 可以。但是如果下雨就不去了。 (Kěyǐ. Dànshì rúguǒ xiàyǔ jiù bù qù le.) Okay. But if it rains we won't go.
> A: 因为周末天气很好。 (Yīnwèi zhōumò tiānqì hěn hǎo.) It's because the weekend weather is great.
> B: 那太棒了。 (Nà tài bàng le.) That's great.

> [!question] Cover and translate: "I put my book on the table."
> 我把书放在桌子上。 — Wǒ bǎ shū fàng zài zhuōzi shàng.

> [!question] Cover and translate: "If you have time, come to my house."
> 如果你有时间，就来我家。 — Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu shíjiān, jiù lái wǒ jiā.

> [!question] Cover and translate: "This dish is spicier than that one."
> 这道菜比那道菜辣。 — Zhè dào cài bǐ nà dào cài là.

## 4. Stretch Material (HSK 3–5, keep for later)

### 4.1 HSK 3 flavor (longer, more specific)
```
我昨天买了一个苹果。  I bought an apple yesterday.
他今天早上六点起床。  He got up at 6 this morning.
她写了一封信。        She wrote a letter.
他没有去参加聚会。    He did not attend the party.
我们需要更多的时间。  We need more time.
```

### 4.2 HSK 4–5 flavor (complex, academic-adjacent)
```
我希望明天可以早点起床。  I hope I can get up early tomorrow.
他经常去图书馆学习。      He often studies at the library.
我的目标是成为一名医生。  My goal is to become a doctor.
我们组织了一次大型讨论。  We organized a large-scale discussion.
他的发明改变了整个世界。  His invention changed the whole world.
```

## 5. Practice Drills (active recall)

> [!question] Fill the blank: 我没____吃饭 (didn't eat)
> 没**有** or just 没**吃**饭。 "I didn't eat" = 我没吃饭。

> [!question] Turn into a past action: 我去图书馆 (went)
> 我去**了**图书馆。

> [!question] Use 把: "He put the phone on the desk."
> 他把手机放在桌子上。

> [!question] Use 比: "I'm taller than you."
> 我比你高。

> [!question] Use 一边…一边…: "I listen to music while doing homework."
> 我一边听音乐，一边做作业。

2. Day 3–4: 把 and 被 (do‑with / passive).
