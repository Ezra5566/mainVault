# HSK 1 — Basics & Survival Chinese

> [!summary] What this note is

## 1. The Mental Model (read this first)

Do **not** study random vocabulary lists or isolated characters. Study **patterns, chunks, and sentence formulas** — like coding syntax.

| Learn THIS way | Not THIS way |
|---|---|
| Whole chunks: 我想吃 (I want to eat) | Isolated word "想" |
| Formulas: `我 + 今天 + 去 + 学校` | Memorizing 150 words flat |
| Reusable components (radicals) | Every character equally |

Most HSK 1 sentences are built from just: **pronoun → time → verb → object**. Master that slot order and you can remix endlessly.

```text
我 今天 去 学校
Wǒ   jīntiān qù xuéxiào
I    today  go school
(I'm going to school today.)
```
Swap any slot: 我**明天**去**饭店** · **他**今天**吃**米饭 · 我**昨天**没去学校.

## 2. Core Grammar Rules

> [!note] Big picture
> Chinese is **SVO** (like English), has **no verb conjugation** (吃 is the same for I/you/he), and **no spaces** between words in writing. Time words usually sit at the front. These three facts make HSK 1 much easier than it looks.

### 2.1 是 (shì) — "to be"
Connects a subject to a noun/description. **Do NOT use 是 before an adjective.**

```
我是学生。      Wǒ shì xuéshēng.        I am a student.
她是老师。      Tā shì lǎoshī.          She is a teacher.
```
> [!warning] Trap
> 我是学生 ✓ (I *am* a student) vs 我学生 ✗. But for adjectives: 我很高兴 ✓ (I'm happy), **never** 我是高兴 ✗.

### 2.2 Questions with 吗 (ma)
Add 吗 at the **end** of a statement to make a yes/no question. Answer 是 (yes) / 不 (no).

```
你好吗？              Nǐ hǎo ma?             How are you?
你学习中文吗？        Nǐ xuéxí Zhōngwén ma? Do you study Chinese?
```

### 2.3 很 (hěn) — "very/quite"
Sits before adjectives. In HSK 1–2 it often just makes the sentence complete — 我很高兴 means "I'm happy," not "I'm *very* happy."

```
他很高兴。  Tā hěn gāoxìng.  He is happy.
```

### 2.4 Negation: 不 (bù) vs 没 (méi)
| Word | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **不 (bù)** | Not, for actions/states (present & future) | 我不学习中文。 I don't study Chinese. |
| **没 (méi)** | Past completion & "don't have" | 我没吃饭。 I didn't eat. / 我没钱。 I don't have money. |

> [!tip] Tone shift
> 不 (bù) becomes **bú** before a 4th‑tone syllable: 不是 → bú shì, 不学 → bú xué.

### 2.5 有 (yǒu) — "to have / there is"
Possession and existence.

```
我有一本书。        Wǒ yǒu yì běn shū.  I have a book.
今天有很多工作。    Jīntiān yǒu hěn duō gōngzuò. There's a lot of work today.
```

### 2.6 Measure words (量词 liàngcí) — the hard one
You **must** put a measure word between a number and a noun. Memorize it *alongside* each noun.

| Measure word | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 个 (gè) | general (people, things, ideas) | 一个人 (one person), 一个朋友 |
| 本 (běn) | books, notebooks | 三本书 (three books) |
| 张 (zhāng) | flat things (paper, tables, tickets) | 两张纸 (two sheets of paper) |
| 条 (tiáo) | long thin things (fish, rivers) | 一条河 |
| 杯 (bēi) | cup | 一杯水 (a cup of water) |
| 只 (zhī) | animals, pairs (birds, cats) | 一只猫 (a cat) |

```
yí gè rén   一个人   one person
sān běn shū 三本书   three books
liǎng zhāng zhǐ 两张纸 two pieces of paper
```

### 2.7 Chunk patterns to memorize (from the HSK 1 drills)
These eight cover most beginner sentences. Get them automatic.

1. **A 是 B** — 我是中国人 · 他是老师 · 她是医生
2. **有** — 我有钱 · 学校有学生
3. **不 + verb/adj** — 我不去 · 我不好
4. **Statement + 吗？** — 你好吗？
5. **这是什么？** — 这=this, 是=is, 什么=what (learn as one chunk)
6. **想 + verb/noun** — 我想吃饭 · 我想去中国
7. **会** (can / know how) — 我会说中文
8. **A 在 B** (location) — 我在学校 · 书在桌子上

## 5. Practice Sentences (HSK 1)

A curated, deduplicated set. Read the Chinese, then the pinyin, then say it aloud. Use the toggles for self‑testing.

> [!question] Can you say "I study Chinese"?
> 我在学习中文。 — wǒ zài xuéxí Zhōngwén

> [!question] "What color do you like?"
> 你喜欢什么颜色？ — nǐ xǐhuān shénme yánsè?

> [!question] "The weather is nice today."
> 今天天气很好。 — jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo

> [!question] "Let's go to the park."
> 我们去公园吧。 — wǒmen qù gōngyuán ba

> [!question] "He runs every day."
> 他每天跑步。 — tā měitiān pǎobù

> [!question] "My hobby is reading."
> 我的兴趣是阅读。 — wǒ de xìngqù shì yuèdú

> [!question] "I can speak a little Chinese."
> 我会说一点中文。 — wǒ huì shuō yīdiǎn Zhōngwén

**Self‑test drill (do these from memory, cover, then check):**
- 这是我的笔。 — This is my pen.
- 他有一只猫。 — He has a cat.
- 我每周去图书馆。 — I go to the library every week.
- 他的车很贵。 — His car is expensive.
- 我喜欢在海边散步。 — I like walking on the beach.

> [!tip] Writing it up
> For each sentence: write it on grid paper, say it aloud, then use the meaning to make *your own* sentence. Production beats recognition.

## 6. The "Fastest Path" Daily System

> [!example] ~120 min/day, optimized for patterns
> 1. **30 min listening** — slow Chinese + HSK 1 dialogues → train pattern recognition.
> 2. **30 min sentence drilling** — repeat *whole sentences* aloud, many times (never isolated words).
> 3. **20 min character recognition** — use radicals/component breakdowns. Recognition > handwriting.
> 4. **20 min active recall** — produce sentences yourself from the templates above (e.g. "I go to school / I don't go today / He wants water").

**Priority order for what to memorize first:**
1. sentence patterns → 2. pronouns → 3. common verbs → 4. question words → 5. time words → 6. numbers → 7. radicals/components. **Not** random nouns.

**Tools:** HelloChinese, Super Chinese, Pleco (dictionary), Anki (spaced recall).

> [!success] The shortcut
> Once you can do 是 / 有 / 不 / 在 / 想 / 会, you understand a large percentage of beginner Chinese. Keep it there.
